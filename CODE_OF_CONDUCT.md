# Code of conduct

Keep collaboration respectful, lawful, and scientifically honest.

- Discuss methods and evidence without attacking people.
- Distinguish reproduced results, hypotheses, and unsupported claims.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.
- Respect model licenses, upstream attribution, and responsible research use.
- Report vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

Maintainers may remove abusive, unsafe, misleading, or license-incompatible
content and restrict participation when necessary.
